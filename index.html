The Game

Build. Rearrange. Survive.

Cloudbreaker is a roguelite, inventory management, survivors-like. Explore a sky-world, finding relics and defeating hordes of enemies.

Unlock and upgrade airships, each with its own unique layout, abilities, and strategies. Place Engine Parts into your ship's grid — strategic positioning creates powerful synergies that define your run.

Descend through cloud layers, discovering ruins and battling enemies called Biotes. Earn resources, upgrade your loadout, and face boss encounters that test every decision you've made.

Features

What Makes Cloudbreaker

🗂️

Inventory as Your Build

Your ship's Engine Grid is an inventory puzzle. Where you place parts matters — adjacency and orientation unlock powerful synergies.

✈️

Multiple Airships

Unlock airships with distinct hull layouts, unique abilities, and individual upgrade trees. Each ship demands a different strategy.

🌩️

Sky-World Exploration

Traverse a vast sky filled with cloud layers, ancient ruins, and islands. Every run reveals new discoveries and dangers.

💥

Horde Combat

Face relentless waves of Biotes in twin-stick bullet-hell combat. Rearrange your build mid-combat to counter what the sky throws at you.

🏆

Boss Encounters

Every run culminates in punishing boss fights that test your build, your skill, and the choices you've made along the way.

🔧

Deep Upgrades

Individual upgrade paths for ships, weapons, and relics. No two runs play the same — theory-craft endless combinations.
