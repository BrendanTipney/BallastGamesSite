Studio

Who We Are

Ballast Games is Brendan Tipney and Anand Friesen, a two-person indie studio based in Nova Scotia, Canada.

We care about tight, satisfying gameplay. Every mechanic should feel good to interact with, every run should feel meaningfully different, and every system should reward thinking.

We aim to build distilled versions of established genres, with a unique twist. Our approach identifies what is fundamentally compelling about a genre and delivers it in a focused, minimalist package.

Brendan Tipney : Code & Design
Anand Friesen : Art & Design
